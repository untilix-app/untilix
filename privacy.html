Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Untilix is a private countdown manager for iPhone. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

The short version

  • All your countdowns are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • No account, sign-up, or login is required.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No data is sent to our servers, because we have none.
  • If you email support, we only use your message to reply.

What data Untilix stores on your device

  • Event details you create: title, date, optional time, emoji, color, category, notes.
  • Settings: theme, language, default sort, "show past events" toggle, reminder sound, custom categories, countdown colors.
  • The data above lives in a local SQLite database inside the iOS app sandbox.

What data Untilix does NOT collect

  • Account information: there is no account.
  • Contact information: we never ask.
  • Location, photos, microphone, camera, health, contacts: we never request these.
  • Usage analytics or diagnostics: none are integrated.
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data: none.

Optional local notifications

Untilix can remind you before a countdown. Reminders are local notifications: scheduled on-device by iOS, with no remote push token and no server involvement. The permission to send notifications is requested only when you enable a reminder on a specific event, and never on first launch. If you deny the permission, the rest of the app continues to work normally.

iCloud and backups

iOS may include app data in your encrypted iPhone or iCloud backups according to your system settings. Untilix does not control this. To erase app data, open iOS Settings, then General, then iPhone Storage, then Untilix, then Delete App. You can also use the in-app Settings, then "Delete All Events" button.

Third parties

Untilix does not share any data with third parties. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it makes no network requests.

Data retention and deletion

Your events stay on your iPhone until you delete them. You can delete a single event from its detail screen, delete everything with Settings, then "Delete All Events", or remove all app data by deleting the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side to request or delete.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We do not sell or share them, and we do not use them for advertising. We may use automated tools to help sort messages and draft replies. To have your messages deleted, email us and ask; we will delete them.

Children

Untilix does not target children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, the change will appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
