Untilix - Support

A private, offline countdown manager for iPhone.

About

Untilix helps you keep track of upcoming moments - birthdays, holidays, trips, exams, anniversaries - with clean, offline-only storage. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no tracking.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a countdown?

Tap the blue "+ Add Event" button on the home screen. Enter a title, date, and any optional details. Tap "Save".

How do I set a reminder?

When creating or editing an event, turn on the "Reminder" switch, then pick when you want to be reminded (2, 6 or 12 hours before, on the day, 1 day, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month or 3 months before). iOS will ask for notification permission the first time only.

My reminder did not fire. What should I check?
  1. Open iOS Settings, then Notifications, then Untilix, and make sure notifications are allowed.
  2. Open Untilix and check Settings, then Notifications; the permission row should say "granted".
  3. If a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode was on, the alert may be waiting in Notification Center instead.
  4. If the event's target date has already passed, the reminder will not fire - reminders for past events are skipped.
How do I delete an event?

Open the event, then tap "Delete" at the bottom. You will be asked to confirm.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings, then the "Data" section, then "Delete All Events". You will be asked to confirm twice. This removes every event and cancels all scheduled reminders on this device.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All events live only on your iPhone. There is no cloud sync and no analytics.

Can I use Untilix on multiple devices?

Not in v1.0. Each iPhone keeps its own local list. Multi-device sync is not a feature of this version.

Does Untilix have a widget or Live Activity?

Not in v1.0. The app opens directly to your countdowns.
